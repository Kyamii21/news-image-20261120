One 1024×1024 front page summing up the world news of Friday 20 November

## Lead story — Russian missiles land in NATO Poland

Two Russian missiles and a suspected jet-powered drone came down in
south-east Poland (Nowy Lipowiec, Rudnik nad Sanem, Mołodycz) during
Russia's largest missile-and-drone strike of November 2026 on Ukraine:
27 ballistic missiles, 84 cruise missiles and 404 Shahed/Gerbera drones.
NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte called it a "grave violation" of Polish
airspace; PM Donald Tusk called it an "intentional provocation". No
injuries were reported in Poland.

## Diplomacy — Trump sells Shenzhen summit

The first-ever meeting of the US, Chinese and Russian leaders (80 minutes,
Shenzhen, 19 Nov) ended in a four-point joint "peace declaration":
"a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought", UN reform, more
refined-petroleum supply, and limits on the ICC. On Friday 20 Nov Trump
pitched the trip at a 90-minute White House press conference.

## Justice — Myanmar guilty of genocide

The International Court of Justice found Myanmar committed genocide
against the Rohingya — the first time in the court's 80-year history a
state has been held responsible for genocide (9 votes to 6; an estimated
10,000 Rohingya killed in the 2017 clearance operations). The ruling was
handed down on 19 Nov; on Friday 20 Nov Myanmar's government dismissed it
as "unfair".

## Europe — UK and EU seal "reset" deals

At Friday's Brussels summit the UK and EU agreed to ease food trade
(estimated £5.1bn a year to the UK economy), link their emissions-trading
systems (£3.8bn a year) and let 100,000 young people a year work or study
on each side for up to three years. Deals apply from 2028.

## Middle East — Israeli strikes kill 6 in Gaza

Israeli air and drone strikes killed six Palestinians in central Gaza on
Friday (four near Bureij, one in Nuseirat, one in a displaced-people's
tent in Deir al-Balah), despite the ceasefire in effect since October
2025. An AFP tally puts Palestinian deaths since the truce began at more
than 1,600.

The COP31 climate conference in Antalya slipped past its Friday deadline

## Disaster — M6.7 quake shakes Peru

A magnitude 6.7 earthquake struck central Peru at 19:09 local time on
Friday, 108 km deep near Cerro de Pasco/Junín. It was felt in Lima;
authorities reported no immediate casualties or structural damage.

## US justice — Palisades arsonist found guilty

A federal jury on Friday convicted Jonathan Rinderknecht, 30, of starting
the January 2025 Palisades Fire at retrial (the first jury deadlocked
10–2). The fire killed 12 people and destroyed some 6,800 structures. He
faces 5 to 40 years; sentencing is set for 17 March 2027.
